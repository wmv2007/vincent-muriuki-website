CUT & CRAFT BARBERSHOP — FONT PACK

Curated for a black-and-gold luxury barbershop website.

Recommended combinations:
1. Inter ExtraBold/Black + Inter Regular — modern, clean, premium
2. Cabin Bold + Inter Regular — warm, contemporary, highly readable
3. EB Garamond Small Caps + Inter — classic luxury / editorial feel
4. Nimbus Sans Narrow Bold + Inter — strong condensed barber-shop headings
5. GFS Didot Bold + Inter — high-fashion luxury accent
6. Lobster Two + Inter — use sparingly for a signature/accent look

Suggested site usage:
- Logo/brand: GFS Didot Bold or EB Garamond Small Caps
- Hero headings: Inter Black or Nimbus Sans Narrow Bold
- Navigation/buttons: Inter SemiBold/Bold
- Body text: Inter Regular
- Small luxury accents: EB Garamond Small Caps

IMPORTANT:
These font files are provided from fonts installed in the working environment.
Before embedding them directly in a production website, verify each font's
license/redistribution terms and use a web-licensed WOFF2 version where possible.
For best website performance, WOFF2 is generally preferred over OTF/TTF.
